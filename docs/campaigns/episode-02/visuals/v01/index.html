03 / One identity, every episode

Production direction

Intro / 1.2 seconds

Input → connection → output.

Two points approach. An orange pulse connects them. The official mark resolves, followed by the episode number. Let a strong hook precede the sting.

Transition / 0.35 seconds

A signal becomes an edge.

A fine orange line crosses the image and carries us into the next scene. Match a weave, fingertip or material edge. Keep sound restrained.

Outro / 3 seconds

Hold the invitation.

Let the final image settle beneath the title. Hold the date, time and location long enough to read. Add one booking CTA when the destination is confirmed.
