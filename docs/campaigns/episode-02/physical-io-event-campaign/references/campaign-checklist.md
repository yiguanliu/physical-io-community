# Recurring campaign checklist

Intake: event title/episode, theme, date/time/timezone, exact venue, registration URL, capacity, audience, programme, confirmed speakers/sponsors, budget, team, brand assets, recordings, permissions and active accounts.

Planning outputs: brief with evidence/unknowns; dated calendar; copy and shot briefs; source ledger with timecodes when reviewed; outreach/channel shortlist; process/results log. Keep one CTA per asset and exact facts consistent across variants.

Previous episode: manifesto answering who we are / why join / what participants get; six recap cuts covering community, motivation, takeaway and three real contributor POVs. Adapt count/subjects to the event. Build or reuse branded intro/outro/transition and caption/lower-third templates.

New episode beats:
1. Call for designers, engineers, researchers and founders.
2. Call for sponsors.
3. Who we are: 30-second abstract teaser.
4. Practical event announcement reel.
5. Confirmed speaker reveal.
6. Topic chapter one.
7. Topic chapter two.
8. Topic chapter three.
9. Signup, where and when.
10. Confirmed sponsor reveal.
11. Verified hiring reveal, if applicable.
12. Consented attendee signal or aggregate disciplines.
13. Relevant channel discovery and outreach preparation (do early).
14. Seven-day countdown.
15. Three-day countdown.
16. 24-hour countdown at actual start-minus-24h.
17. Event-day arrival post.
18. Live stories with accurate captions.
19. Finishing post.
20. Thanks and credits.
21. Recap.
22. Approved testimonial.
23. Community member interview.
24. Full YouTube video if produced.
25. Spotify episode if produced and intelligible without visuals.
26. Speaker cuts with preserved context.
27. Feedback conversations and lessons.

Default cadence: four to six weeks preparation, three weeks awareness/reveals, final-week conversion, event-day capture, four weeks follow-through. Compress to available time; use Stories or combine related beats rather than flooding feeds.

Sponsor flow: verify entity and fit → source personalisation → define proposed contribution/deliverables → draft → send only with explicit authority → record → stop follow-ups on reply/decline/opt-out. Never turn prospects into confirmed partners.

Event capture: assign audio/camera/story owner; check access, equipment and consent; preserve clean originals; capture full talks, context, approved reactions and three member interviews; back up and review.

Measure registrations and checked-in attendance with explicit denominators, relevant engagement, sponsor progress and feedback. Set numerical targets from capacity/baseline rather than inventing them. Keep source provenance and actual publish URLs. Update process log at each phase and extract reusable lessons after event.

## Visual production workflow
When asked for storyboard/post visuals: inspect existing brand assets first; create distinct shot prompts with time, camera, sound, narration and transition; use available image-generation tooling to generate concept art; preserve exact prompts and original files. Keep typography and event facts editable in HTML overlays and use the official logo. Reuse keyframes across coherent post variants. Present shots, captions and post layouts in an offline-capable HTML gallery with all local dependencies. Offer full-size layouts and PNG exports. Verify desktop/narrow layouts, image loading, caption disclosure and exports. Record generated versus documentary provenance, unresolved facts and export dimensions. Never depict generated scenes as actual event footage.
