# Physical I/O — Episode 02: Love, Mind + Body

Campaign working pack · 14 September 2026 · Draft, not published

## The idea
Physical I/O is a community led by curiosity, design and engineering, exploring how humans and intelligent systems coexist in the physical world. Episode 01 establishes the people and purpose. Episode 02 asks: **What happens when intelligence gets a body?**

Build this as a cinematic anthology: a recognisable opening, a new central question each event, recurring human perspectives, and an ending that opens the next episode. Love, Death + Robots is the user's creative reference for anthology structure and cinematic range. Use Physical I/O’s own identity, footage, sound and typography.

**Love:** connection, expression, gesture, how we relate to machines.
**Intelligence:** perception, computation, tools and learning.
**Body:** fabric, force, materials, movement and physical constraints.

The audience promise: meet people working across disciplines, exchange practical questions, and find possible collaborators. Do not promise employment, investment, demos or access to named organisations without confirmation.
